WHO WE ARE

Deaf youth.
A shared future.

Deaf Youth Canada / Jeunes Sourds du Canada is led by Deaf youth and serves Deaf residents of Canada and Canadians aged 18 to 30. We bring young people together through social, educational, and community opportunities.

From leadership camp to connections across provinces, DYC / JSC encourages the exchange of experiences and works with Deaf organizations to support youth inclusion. We operate under CAD-ASC.
